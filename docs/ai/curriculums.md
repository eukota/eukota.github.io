# AI Curriculums

Material for learning to understand and use AI, for anyone.

## Where it started

The first design was four phases, each with its own levels:

- **Phase A, Foundation.** For people who are hesitant or skeptical about AI. It takes their concerns seriously and makes the case for why learning AI matters.
- **Phase B, General Capability.** Practical skills for any motivated learner: asking better questions, breaking work into pieces an AI can help with, verifying what it produces, and building workflows.
- **Phase C, Engineering Fundamentals.** For engineers already committed to AI, built around Steve Yegge's eight stages of how developers' use of AI evolves, from occasional help to orchestrating many agents. His article is [Welcome to Gas Town](https://steve-yegge.medium.com/welcome-to-gas-town-4f25ee16dd04).
- **Phase D, Frontier.** Systems still taking shape: tools, observability, context, guardrails, and agent infrastructure, along with interview preparation.

That material is in my [ai-projects repository](https://github.com/eukota/ai-projects/tree/main/projects/teaching-ai).

## Where it is now

I'm rebuilding it. A ladder of levels assumes one right order and a tool landscape that has since changed a lot. The new version has no prerequisites, and your own goal decides what you learn. It's organized as a map of five overlapping areas instead of a staircase:

- Society and humanity
- Everyday literacy
- Tools and agents
- Building and deploying
- Agentic systems

You can browse by topic or start from something you want to do. Each lesson comes with a reusable skill you can give your AI.

It's early. One lesson is written in full and the rest are outlines.
